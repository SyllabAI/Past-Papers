## Unit1

Mechanics

Kinematic equations of motion

$$
v = u + a t
$$

$$
s = u t + \frac {1}{2} a t ^ {2}
$$

$$
v ^ {2} = u ^ {2} + 2 a s
$$

Forces

$$
\Sigma F = m a
$$

$$
g = F / m
$$

$$
W = m g
$$

Work and energy

$$
\Delta W = F \Delta s
$$

$$
E _ {\mathrm {k}} = \frac {1}{2} m v ^ {2}
$$

$$
\Delta E _ {\mathrm {g r a v}} = m g \Delta h
$$

Materials

Stokes' law

$$
F = 6 \pi \eta r v
$$

Hooke's law

$$
F = k \Delta x
$$

Density

$$
\rho = m / V
$$

Pressure

$$
p = F / A
$$

Young modulus

$$
E = \sigma / \varepsilon \mathrm {w h e r e}
$$

$$
\mathrm {S t r e s s} \sigma = F / A
$$

$$
\mathrm {S t r a i n} \varepsilon = \Delta x / x
$$

Elastic strain energy

$$
E _ {\mathrm {e l}} = \frac {1}{2} F \Delta x
$$
