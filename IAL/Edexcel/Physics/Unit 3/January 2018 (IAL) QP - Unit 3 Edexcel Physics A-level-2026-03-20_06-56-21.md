## Mechanics

Kinematic equations of motion

$$
v = u + a t
$$

$$
s = u t + \frac {1}{2} a t ^ {2}
$$

$$
v ^ {2} = u ^ {2} + 2 a s
$$

Forces

$ \Sigma F=ma $

g = F/m

W=mg

Work and energy

$$
\Delta W = F \Delta s
$$

$$
E _ {k} = 1 / 2 m v ^ {2}
$$

$$
\Delta \mathrm {E} _ {\mathrm {g r a v}} = \mathrm {m g} \Delta \mathrm {h}
$$

## Materials

Stokes' law

F=6piηrv

Hooke's law

F=kΔx

Density

$$
\rho = m / V
$$

Pressure

p = F/A

Young modulus

E= $ \sigma /\varepsilon $ where

$$
\mathrm {S t r e s s} \sigma = \mathrm {F} / \mathrm {A}
$$

$$
\mathrm {S t r a i n} \varepsilon = \Delta \mathrm {x} / \mathrm {x}
$$

Elastic strain energy

$$
E _ {\mathrm {e l}} = 1 / 2 F \Delta x
