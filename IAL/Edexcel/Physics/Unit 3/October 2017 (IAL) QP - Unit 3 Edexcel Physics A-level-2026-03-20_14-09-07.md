## Mechanics

Kinematic equations of motion

$$
v = u + a t
$$

$$
s = u t + \frac {1}{2} a t ^ {2}
$$

$$
v ^ {2} = u ^ {2} + 2 a s
$$

$ \Sigma F=ma $

Forces

g = F/m

W=mg

Work and energy

$$
\Delta W = F \Delta s
$$

$$
E _ {k} = 1 / 2 m v ^ {2}
$$

$$
\Delta \mathrm {E} _ {\mathrm {g r a v}} = \mathrm {m g} \Delta \mathrm {h}
$$

Density

Pressure
