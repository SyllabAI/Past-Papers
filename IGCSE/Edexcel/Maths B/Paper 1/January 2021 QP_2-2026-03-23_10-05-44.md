26 A particle P is moving along a straight line. At time t seconds, the displacement, s metres, of P from a fixed point O on the line is given by

$$
s = 5 t ^ {2} - t ^ {3} \quad 0 \leqslant t \leqslant 5
$$

At time T seconds, where T>0,P comes to instantaneous rest.

(a) Find the exact value of T.

(b) Find the total distance travelled by the particle between t=0 and t=5
